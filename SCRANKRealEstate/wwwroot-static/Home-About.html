About Chandler Real Estate

Your trusted partner in Idaho real estate since 2010

Our Story

Chandler Real Estate was founded with a simple mission: to help families find their perfect home in beautiful Idaho. With over 15 years of experience in the local market, we've helped thousands of clients buy, sell, and invest in Idaho properties.

Our team of dedicated real estate professionals brings extensive knowledge of Idaho's diverse communities, from the vibrant city life of Boise to the serene mountain towns of Sun Valley and everything in between.

We pride ourselves on providing personalized service, honest advice, and unwavering commitment to our clients' success. Whether you're a first-time homebuyer or an experienced investor, we're here to guide you every step of the way.
