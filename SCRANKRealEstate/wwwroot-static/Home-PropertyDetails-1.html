Property Description

Experience the perfect blend of country living and modern comfort on this beautiful 5- acre property, with 4 acres currently planted in hay. This spacious 3,888 sq ft single level home offers room to spread out, featuring multiple living areas and two cozy fireplaces, one propane and one wood burning stove. Designed for both relaxation and functionality, the property includes a private indoor pool house, ideal for year round enjoyment, along with an exterior toilet room for added convenience during outdoor entertaining. Inside you will find thoughtful upgrades throughout, including built in wet bar with beautiful cabinetry, glass shelving and built in wine bottle holder. For all your storage and hobby needs, enjoy a 30x50 insulted shop, a spacious RV garage with ample storage, an attached flex room ideal for gym, office, or hobby space, and an additional pantry area in the garage.
