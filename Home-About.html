About the Chandler Real Estate Team

Your trusted partner in Idaho real estate since 1999

Julie Chandler

Let me introduce myself. I have lived in the Treasure Valley my whole life. I have been married to my husband Ernie since 1986. We have a successful farming operation, baking business and I have been a dedicated realtor since 1999. I have raised 2 boys, my oldest is part of our farming operations. My youngest son lives in Iowa.

My Family is my cornerstone of my life. Having a very supportive husband, 2 grown sons, 2 cherished daughters-in-laws, and 2 delightful grandchildren my world is enriched by the love and connections that bind it all together. Balancing the roles of a dedicated realtor, a baker and a family person I find fulfillment in both professional achievements and the joys of family milestones.

Collaborating with my daughter-in-law as my business partner adds a unique and familial dynamic to my professional journey. Together we navigate the real estate and the baking business blending our skills to create a powerful and harmonious team. Our partnership not only strengthens our business but also deepens the bonds within my family, creating a shared success story.

Throughout my real estate career, my unwavering commitment extends beyond transactions. My passion lies in helping people find not just a property, but a place they can truly call home. In my endeavors, I have discovered that the true essence of success is in the meaningful connections forged and the joy of guiding others toward their dream homes or properties.

I have diverse experience in real estate by success in listing/selling residential, farmland and commercial properties. With my understanding of various property types, it gives me the ability to cater to a wider range of clients' needs.

Outside of work, I embrace the tranquility of living on the farm and co-founded 2 Farm Wives Baking, infusing a taste of passion into my pursuits. Golfing, running, traveling, quilting, spending quality time with my grandchildren and staying healthy and fit round out my active lifestyle, adding balance to my vibrant journey.

Bailey Chandler

As a devoted wife, mom of two amazing kids, and proud farm wife, family has always been at the heart of everything I do. Between coaching volleyball, cheering on my kids at their sporting events, spending time outdoors, enjoying power sports, and baking alongside my mother-in-law for our baking business, 2 Farm Wives Baking, I know just how important it is to have a place that truly feels like home.

For the last couple of years, I’ve had the privilege of helping families, first-time buyers, sellers, and dreamers navigate one of the biggest milestones in their lives — finding the perfect property to fit their needs and lifestyle. Whether it’s helping clients buy, sell, invest, or transform a house into a forever home, I am passionate about making the process personal, smooth, and rewarding.

I believe real estate is more than just transactions — it’s about relationships, trust, and advocating for the people I serve every step of the way. My goal is to make every client feel supported, valued, and confident throughout their journey. I’m committed to working hard, communicating honestly, and helping my clients achieve their dreams while creating lasting connections along the way.
