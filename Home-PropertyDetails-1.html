Property Description

Experience the perfect blend of country living and modern comfort on this beautiful 5- acre property, with 4 acres currently planted in hay. This spacious 3,888 sq ft single level home offers room to spread out, featuring multiple living areas and two cozy fireplaces, one propane and one wood burning stove. Designed for both relaxation and functionality, the property includes a private indoor pool house, ideal for year round enjoyment, along with an exterior toilet room for added convenience during outdoor entertaining. Inside you will find thoughtful upgrades throughout, including built in wet bar with beautiful cabinetry, glass shelving and built in wine bottle holder. Updated LED energy efficient lighting fixtures enhance the homes modern feel, while a propane tankless water heater ensures endless hot water. The home also features a recently updated bathroom with a stylish walk in shower. Updated exterior paint completed in 2024 on both the home and RV garage adds to the property's curb appeal. For all your storage and hobby needs, enjoy a 30x50 insulted shop, a spacious RV garage with ample storage, an attached flex room ideal for gym, office, or hobby space, and an additional pantry area in the garage. This property offers the space, upgrades, and versatility you have been looking for ! Don't miss your chance to enjoy peaceful country living with all the extras!
